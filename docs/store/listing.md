## Description (up to 10,000 characters)

Dynamic Isle puts a small, smart island at the top-centre of your Windows screen. It stays out of the way until something happens, then shows you what matters: the song that's playing, a timer counting down, your next meeting, or an AI coding agent that needs your OK.

Rest the mouse on the top edge of the screen and a sheet of quick tools slides down. Move away and it's gone. Clicks pass straight through everywhere else, and it never steals your keyboard.

MUSIC AND VIDEO
See what's playing in Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music in a browser and other apps, with the cover, sound bars that move with the music, and play, pause, skip and seek. Open the player for a big cover and lyrics that scroll along with the song.

CALENDAR
A month view with your events, right in the island. Add events with any start and end time, and get a heads-up a few minutes before each one, with a Join button for Meet, Teams and Zoom links. Optionally connect a Google or Microsoft calendar, or any calendar link.

TIMERS, ALARMS AND FOCUS
Timer and stopwatch, alarms for weekdays or weekends, Focus (Pomodoro) sessions with breaks, and reminders you can type in plain words, like "call mom in 20 min".

AI CODING AGENTS
See what your coding agent is doing: a live timer, the change it's making, and its progress. Allow or deny its requests right from the island, without hunting for the terminal window. Connect agents from Settings in one click.

CALLS AND PRIVACY
A call timer while any app uses your microphone, one shortcut that mutes your microphone in every app at once, and small dots that show when the microphone, camera or screen capture is in use.

QUICK TOOLS
Clipboard history with pins, a quick note, a shelf that holds files while you find where they go, and a converter for images, audio and video, all on your PC.

SYSTEM POP-UPS
Clean pop-ups for volume, brightness, Caps Lock and charging, in the island instead of Windows' own.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No sign-in. No ads. No tracking. The app collects no data: your settings, notes, reminders and clipboard stay on your PC. A few optional features (lyrics, finding missing covers, connecting a calendar) contact a service only when you use them, and each one can be turned off. The privacy policy lists exactly what they send.

Every feature can be switched off, and you choose which tools appear in the sheet and in what order.
