# Dynamic Isle – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 15 November 2026*

Dynamic Isle is a free app that shows music, timers, your calendar and other information at the top of your Windows screen. This policy explains, plainly, what happens with your data.

## The short version

- **We don't collect any data.** Dynamic Isle has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Nothing about you or how you use the app is ever sent to us.
- **Everything stays on your PC.** Your settings, alarms, reminders, notes, calendar events, clipboard history and file shelf are stored only on your computer.
- **No login is required.** The app works fully without signing in to anything.
- A few **optional** features talk to an outside service to do their job. They're listed below, with exactly what they send, and each one can be turned off.

## What is stored on your PC

The app keeps its data in your Windows user folder (`%APPDATA%\dynamic-island`) and in the app's own storage. This includes your settings, alarms, reminders, your note, the list of files on the shelf, and calendar events you add.

- **Clipboard history** (if turned on) is saved **encrypted** with your Windows account, so only you can read it. Copies made by password managers are never recorded. You can turn off "Keep clipboard history after a restart", or turn clipboard history off completely, in Settings.
- **Sign-in tokens** for an optional calendar account (see below) are stored **encrypted** with your Windows account.

Uninstalling the app removes the app itself. You can delete your data folder at any time from Settings › About › Your data.

## Optional features that use the internet

None of these is needed for the app to work. Each one only runs when you use it.

| Feature | When it runs | What is sent, and to whom | How to turn it off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lyrics** | Only when you open the lyrics drawer in the player | The song's title, artist, album and length, once per song, to [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) (and, if LRCLIB has nothing, to [lyrics.ovh](https://lyrics.ovh)). If you add your own Musixmatch key, also to Musixmatch. | Settings › Media › Lyrics |
| **Finding missing covers** | When a song plays without a cover | The song's title and artist, once per song, to Apple's iTunes Search, Deezer and MusicBrainz | Settings › Media › Find missing covers |
| **Calendar accounts** | Only if you choose to connect Google or Microsoft | You sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own page in your browser; the app never sees your password. The app then reads your calendar (and, if you turn on reminder sync, your tasks) **directly** from Google or Microsoft. Nothing passes through any server of ours. | Settings › Calendar › Remove the account |
| **Calendar links (.ics)** | Only if you add one | The app downloads the calendar file from the link you gave | Settings › Calendar › Remove |

These services have their own privacy policies. The app sends them only what is listed above: never your name, email address, files or anything else.

## Things that never leave your PC

- **Microphone, camera and screen-capture indicators:** the app reads Windows' own record of which apps are using them, to show the dots and the call timer. It never turns on or records your microphone or camera.
- **Sound bars:** to make the bars follow your music, the app measures the loudness of your PC's sound output. The sound is never recorded or saved, and it never leaves your PC. Turn it off in Settings › Media.
- **AI agent status:** coding agents you connect report to the app over a connection that only your own PC can reach (`127.0.0.1`).
- **Clipboard, notes, files on the shelf and file conversion:** all handled on your PC.

## Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
